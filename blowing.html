Blowing Exercises

Exercise 1

Take a ball place it on the table, and make the ball move by blowing it with a straw.

Exercise 2

Blowing a balloon is a great activity to strengthen oral motor skills.

Exercise 3

Practice blowing bubbles for fun and exercise.

Exercise 4

Drip a dot of paint on your paper. Now take your straw in your mouth and position it about above the paint and blow to create unique shapes and structures.

Exercise 5

Take a feather and try to keep it in the sky by blowing some air .

Exercise 6

Blowing pinwheel helps you regulate breath control.
